💐 Pick Your Bouquet 💐

The Royal Knight's Finest Arrangements for Her Highness

The art of giving a Bouquet is a simple, timeless gesture and a little way of saying I was out here in the world and thought of you for a second. No occasion needed, no grand reason required. Just something bright and alive, handed from one person to another ✨

Since Her Highness is more of a foodie than a flower girl, the Royal Knight presents these fine arrangements he handpicked himself for her majesty from the finest kingdom gardens and ready to be cooked into something magnificent 😋

The Cilantro Bouquet
Royal Favourite

Reviewed and personally approved by Her Highness, who once declared it made the whole dish 10x the taste and look ✨. Your knight has been ordering extra ever since.

🍛 Dal tadka  ·  🥘 Chicken curry  ·  🫙 Sambar Garnish  ·  🍚 Chicken Biryani

The Carrot Bouquet
Underrated Royalty

Her Highness confessed she has not had gajar ka halwa since 2024 and misses it. The kingdom has reviewed its supply chain. A new vendor is now available and accepting orders.

🍮 Gajar ka halwa  ·  🥕 Gajar achar  ·  🥣 Carrot soup  ·  🥗 Salads

The Cauliflower Bouquet
The Crown Jewel

Regal, versatile and shaped like a crown. Also submitted in partial settlement of your knight's outstanding fibre debt, as Her Highness has been eating his share of salads since March and the balance remains unpaid.

🥘 Aloo gobi  ·  🍛 Gobi manchurian  ·  🫓 Gobi paratha  ·  🍲 Gobi curry

The Onion Bouquet
The One That Makes You Cry

Source of the only tears this warrior has ever admitted to, having fought many battles with fire, knives and onions. Comes with 2 or 3 tissues, though Her Highness has demanded the entire box, on the grounds that Royal Orange Cats do not cry, they plan revenge.

🍛 Every curry ever  ·  🥘 Biryani  ·  🧅 Onion bhaji  ·  🫙 Chutney

The Mirchi Bouquet
For the Bold Palate

Last deployed at 18 units instead of the usual 12, purely for the colour of Biryani. Casualties: one knight, six big scoops of ice cream, after every meal, for a week. Team Extra Spicy has since revised its estimates.

🍳 Spicy Sambar  ·  🥘 Sabzi tadka  ·  🫙 Chutney  ·  🍢 Mirchi Bonda

The Fruits Bouquet
The Royal Spread

Why pick one when you can have it all? Her Highness does not compromise, and honestly, fair enough. Also the only item on this page that will not make her cry, sweat or settle a debt.

🥗 Fruit chaat  ·  🍹 Fresh juice  ·  🍨 Fruit custard  ·  🫙 Mixed jam
