💐 Pick Your Bouquet 💐

The Royal Knight's Finest Arrangements for Her Highness

The art of giving flowers is a simple, timeless gesture and a little way of saying I was out here in the world and thought of you for a second. No occasion needed, no grand reason required. Just something bright and alive, handed from one person to another.

Since Her Highness is more of a foodie than a flower girl, the Royal Knight presents these fine arrangements he handpicked himself for her majesty from the finest kingdom gardens and ready to be cooked into something magnificent 😋

The Cilantro Bouquet
Royal Favourite

The humble hero that elevates every dish it touches. No Indian kitchen is complete without this green blessing.

🍛 Dal tadka  ·  🥘 Chicken curry  ·  🫙 Green chutney  ·  🍚 Biryani garnish

The Carrot Bouquet
Underrated Royalty

Bold, orange, and deeply misunderstood. The carrot bouquet is for the true connoisseur who knows dessert can also be a vegetable.

🍮 Gajar ka halwa  ·  🥕 Gajar achar  ·  🥣 Carrot soup  ·  🥗 Salads

The Cauliflower Bouquet
The Crown Jewel

Regal, versatile, and shaped like a crown — fitting for Her Highness. Underestimated by many, beloved by those who truly know their food.

🥘 Aloo gobi  ·  🍛 Gobi manchurian  ·  🫓 Gobi paratha  ·  🍲 Gobi curry

The Green Chilli Bouquet
For the Bold Palate

This bouquet is not for the faint of heart. A gift for those who believe every meal needs a little heat and a lot of drama.

🍳 Mirchi ka salan  ·  🥘 Sabzi tadka  ·  🫙 Thecha chutney  ·  🍢 Stuffed mirchi

The Mixed Fruits Bouquet
The Royal Spread

Why pick one when you can have it all? This bouquet is for Her Highness who refuses to compromise and honestly, fair enough.

🥗 Fruit chaat  ·  🍹 Fresh juice  ·  🍨 Fruit custard  ·  🫙 Mixed jam
